## 1.1 A Formula {#sec-1-1}

This is a synthetic sample, safe to redistribute. It exercises the deterministic
math gate, the in-book contents page, equation numbering, and MathML output.

$$
x^2 + y^2 = z^2 \qquad (1)
$$

A second, unnumbered display shows that formula objects do not need a printed
number:

$$
a + b = b + a
$$
